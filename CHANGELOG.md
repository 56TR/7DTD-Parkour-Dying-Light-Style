# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Moves: mantle, slide, low slide and crawl, slide jump, landing roll, vault, ledge hang (shimmy, outer and inner
  corners, climb up, drop to a lower ledge), wall climb, wall run, wall jump, coyote jump, air momentum.
- Zombies: slide tackle (hits the whole group), dropkick, stomp, zombie vault.
- Options > General > Parkour: every move and effect can be switched off.
- Parkour perk integration: moves unlock per perk level, optional removal of the perk's jump bonus and of the
  level 5 fall immunity, Auto-Roll at Agility Mastery 4, perk descriptions follow the settings.
- First-person body during moves, camera effects, third-person animations (seen by other players with the mod).
- Multiplayer: client-side, works on servers without the mod.
- 13 languages.
